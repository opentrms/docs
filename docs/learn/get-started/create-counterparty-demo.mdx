In this walkthrough we'll create a new counterparty in the OpenTRMS Workbench, and make it available for trading.

Counterparties live under Reference data in the left-hand navigation.

Start with the legal name. Use the full registered name exactly as it appears on the entity's incorporation documents — not a trading alias.

Next, choose the counterparty type. The type drives which credit and margin rules the risk engine will apply, so it matters.

Set the country of incorporation. This is the jurisdiction of the legal entity, which is not always where the desk you deal with is located.

Now the Legal Entity Identifier — twenty alphanumeric characters. The Workbench validates the check digits before the record can be saved.

Enter the credit limit in US dollars. This is the gross notional cap across every desk, and it's enforced at the point of trade capture.

Finally, attach a standing settlement instruction. Without one, trades against this counterparty will book but will not settle.

Save the counterparty. The record is validated, written, and an audit entry is raised against your user.

The confirmation gives you the new counterparty reference. The record is active immediately and can now be selected during deal entry.

</Walkthrough>
