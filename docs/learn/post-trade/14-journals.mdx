In this walkthrough we'll follow trading activity into the accounting ledger, in the OpenTRMS Workbench.

Every trade eventually becomes an accounting entry. The Journal List is where those entries live, and it is the bridge between the trading floor and the ledger.

Entries carry a type. Mark to market records a change in value, realized profit and loss records a gain or loss that has actually crystallised.

Status matters just as much. A draft entry has been generated but not committed to the ledger, and only posted entries count towards the accounts.

The filters answer the two questions people actually ask — what happened in this accounting period, and what did this particular deal generate.

Opening an entry shows its lines. Every entry balances, so the lines always show a debit against a matching credit.

None of this is typed by hand. Posting Rules is the configuration that generates it — for each product and entry type, which account is debited and which is credited.
